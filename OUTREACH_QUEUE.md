### Directory description (under 400 characters)

Vindla is an iPhone meditation app for moments generic recordings do not fit. Tell Mimir what is on your mind, choose your time and one of eight guide styles, and get a session shaped around that moment. It also includes breathwork, sleep stories, soundscapes, Siri shortcuts, and a simple timer. Free to start, no account required, with practice history stored on-device.

I made a no-install [written Mimir preview](https://vindla-three.vercel.app/try-mimir?campaign=creator_outreach) so you can choose a moment and guide before downloading; the examples are clearly labeled as prewritten samples. Hi [name] — one gentle follow-up in case the one-minute reset is useful for [their audience/use case]. If the product is not a fit, no reply is needed and I will not follow up again. If you tried it, one sentence of blunt feedback would genuinely help.

## Weekly operating rules

- Send 5 deeply personalized pitches per weekday, maximum 25 per week.
- Reference one real piece of the recipient's work; never fake familiarity.
- Follow up once after seven days, then stop.
- Never request or reward a positive App Store review.
- Ask permission before reusing a creator's words, image, or clip.
- Record: sent date, reply, trial started, content published, App Store downloads during the link window, and qualitative feedback.
- Kill rule: if 25 messages produce fewer than 3 replies, rewrite the opener before sending another batch.
- Scale rule: if a published demo produces at least 10 downloads, recruit three more creators in the same audience/use-case niche.
