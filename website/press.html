Vindla is an iPhone meditation app that turns how someone feels right now into a guided session in their choice of four distinct guide styles or a guide they create themselves.

The idea

Generic libraries rarely match a specific moment.

Three minutes before a difficult meeting, after a tense conversation, or when the mind will not settle at night: those moments call for different words and a different tone.

Vindla lets someone describe the moment, choose their available time and preferred guide, and receive a structured meditation from Mimir. The app launched quietly, then its welcome, sharing, and first-session experience were rebuilt around making that first useful minute easier to reach.
