## One sentence

Vindla is an iPhone meditation app that turns how someone feels right now into a guided session in their choice of eight distinct guide styles.

## Short description

Generic recording libraries often fail at the exact moment someone wants help: three minutes before a difficult meeting, after an argument, or when their mind will not settle at night. Vindla lets someone describe that moment, choose their available time and preferred guide, and receive a personalized meditation from Mimir. It also includes breathwork, sleep stories, soundscapes, Siri shortcuts, reminders, and a simple meditation timer.

Vindla is free to start, requires no account, and keeps practice history on-device.

## Founder story

Vindla launched quietly on 2026-04-13 and only nine people found it in its first four months. Instead of pretending that was a successful launch, the product was rebuilt around the biggest points of friction: a six-step welcome became three steps, sharing moved to a reliable web handoff, the website now demonstrates the promise before asking for an install, and review requests wait until a person has completed several meaningful sessions. Version 1.0.2 is the first release built for a real launch rather than a silent one.

## Three demo prompts

1. `I have three minutes before a difficult meeting and cannot stop rehearsing it.`
2. `I am tired but my mind is still moving too quickly to sleep.`
3. `I keep switching between tasks and need to settle into one thing.`

For comparison content, run the same prompt with **Zen Minimalist** and **Warm Guardian**. The product difference is clearest when the situation stays constant and the guide personality changes.

## Suggested coverage angles

### For wellness creators

Can personalization make a short meditation easier to begin than browsing a large recording library?

### For productivity creators

A three-minute mental transition before a meeting or deep-work block, shaped around the user's actual context.

### For ADHD and overthinking creators

Reducing choice overload by asking for one plain-language description and preselecting a guide; this is a usability angle, not a clinical claim.

### For indie-app publications

The honest nine-user launch: what the founder changed before trying distribution again.

### For Apple editorial

A privacy-first, native iPhone practice that combines personalized meditation, distinct guide personalities, Siri, Live Activities, on-device history, and accessible short-session entry points.

## Claims to avoid

Do not claim that Vindla treats anxiety, ADHD, insomnia, depression, trauma, or any medical condition. Describe experiences and product behavior: short reset, guided attention, easier starting point, calmer moment, bedtime wind-down, or personalized practice.

Do not describe Mimir as a therapist, clinician, medical device, or replacement for professional care.
